How it turned

    How Backfire works

    You are not building the deadliest pathogen. You are betting on how humanity reacts. Traps must be armed before their trigger exists — once treatment or cure research crosses the line, it is too late to buy the trap that exploits it.

    Each turn

    1. Read the brief: bloc intentions, event odds, cure forecast.
    2. Spend Drift on Spread, Traps or Influence — one pool, three competing tracks.
    3. End the turn: spread, bloc decisions, an event, trap checks, cure progress.
    4. Read the report to see what changed and why.

    You win when

    Collapse reaches 100 — global infection with no countermeasure left standing.

    You lose if

    • Cure progress reaches 100.
    • Cooperation stays at 80 or above for 6 turns.
    • The outbreak burns out entirely from over-lethality.

    Worth knowing

    • Collapse only grows while infection is above 25% — below that the world rebuilds.
    • Only two traps can be held at once, and holding one costs 10% transmission.
    • The enquiry at 100 rules the outbreak natural: blame evaporates, cooperation jumps and the cure speeds up permanently. Bury evidence to slow it.
    • The Red Bloc hides case numbers until its own outbreak passes 25%. That slows the cure, but your map data for Russia, Central Asia and East Asia reads ≈ unverified.
    • Summits add cooperation unless you sabotage them beforehand.
