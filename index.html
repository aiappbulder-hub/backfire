      How Backfire works

      You are not trying to build the deadliest pathogen. You are betting on how humanity reacts. Traps must be armed before their trigger exists — once treatment or cure research crosses the line, it is too late to buy the trap that exploits it.

      Each turn

      1. Read the briefing: region changes, bloc intentions, event odds, cure forecast.
      2. Spend Drift on Spread, Traps or Influence — one pool, three competing tracks.
      3. End the turn: spread, bloc decisions, an event, trap checks, cure progress.
      4. Read the turn report to see what changed and why.

      You win when

      Collapse reaches 100 — global infection with no countermeasure left standing.

      You lose if

      • Cure progress reaches 100.
      • Cooperation stays at 80 or above for 6 turns.
      • The outbreak burns out entirely from over-lethality.

      Things worth knowing

      • Only two traps can be held at once, and holding one costs 10% transmission.
      • Investigation at 100 means investigators rule the outbreak natural: blame evaporates, cooperation jumps and the cure speeds up permanently. Bury evidence to slow it.
      • The Red Bloc conceals case numbers until its own outbreak passes 25%. That slows the joint cure, but your map data for its regions is unverified (≈).
      • Summits add cooperation unless you sabotage them beforehand.
      • The cure forecast is an estimate from the latest research rate, not a promise.
